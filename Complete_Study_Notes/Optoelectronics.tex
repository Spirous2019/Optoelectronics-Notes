\begin{center}
    {\large\bfseries A Note Before You Read}\\[0.4em]
    {\color{rulegray}\rule{0.35\textwidth}{0.5pt}}
\end{center}

\vspace{1.2cm}

\noindent
As someone navigating ADHD and significant memory challenges, I have often found traditional studying to be an uphill battle. Optoelectronics, in particular, was a course where I hit a wall. While scattered handwritten notes existed, none offered the underlying \textit{physical intuition} I craved. Mathematical steps would leap forward without justification, leaving me stranded and disconnected from the very concepts I was so fascinated by.

\vspace{0.6em}
\noindent
Standard AI chatbots—which had been lifesavers in other subjects—could not keep up with the fragmented, specialized nature of this material. So, I took a different approach. I gathered every handwritten note and resource I could find and built a dedicated AI workspace. I instructed it to bridge the gaps, illuminate the grey areas, generate rigorous plots, and explicitly answer the \textit{"why"} behind every \textit{"how"}.

\vspace{0.6em}
\noindent
The result is the document you are reading now. It was not written to be the shortest summary, but the most \textit{honest} companion. It was designed to make physics genuinely enjoyable for those of us who need a logical, unbroken narrative to learn effectively. If you are a student who grasps these concepts naturally, I wish you nothing but the best. But if you are someone whose mind works like mine, I hope you find this to be a treasure.

\vspace{0.6em}
\noindent
Because syllabus structures often change, the entire \LaTeX\ source code and Python plotting scripts are available open-source. You are free to reorder, adapt, or correct this material to suit your needs:

\vspace{0.5em}

\end{center}

\vspace{0.8em}
\noindent
All I ask in return for this work is a sincere Du\={a}' for me, as life has been quite heavy lately. I wish everyone reading this peace, clarity, and success.
