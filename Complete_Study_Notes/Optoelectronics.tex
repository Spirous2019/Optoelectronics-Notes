\vspace{1.2cm}

\noindent
Optoelectronics is a course where learning resources are notoriously limited and scattered—there are no previously recorded lectures, unified textbooks matched to the syllabus, or comprehensive study guides. For someone managing ADHD, attempting to build physical intuition from fragmented handwritten notes and sudden mathematical leaps was an immense challenge.

\vspace{0.6em}
\noindent
To solve this, I gathered every available resource and leveraged a 1-year Google Gemini Pro subscription for students alongside Google's Antigravity AI workspace to build these dedicated notes. The objective was to construct a rigorous, self-contained reference where every derivation step is filled in, every physical mechanism is explained from first principles, and critical scientific concepts are explicitly highlighted—ensuring deep conceptual understanding over rote memorization.

\vspace{0.6em}
\noindent
I am confident that this document is thorough enough to serve as a complete standalone guide for the course. Whether you missed lectures or simply learn best through a structured, logical narrative, these notes were designed to provide the physical clarity that standard materials often omit.

\vspace{0.6em}
\noindent
Because course outlines often change in topic sequence or content—whether due to a change in professors or an updated teaching plan—the complete \LaTeX\ source code and Python plotting scripts are open-source and freely available. Feel free to reorder the material, correct any errors you spot, add or omit sections, and adapt everything to your needs:

\vspace{0.5em}
\begin{center}
    \url{https://github.com/Spirous2019/Optoelectronics-Notes.git}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.8em}
